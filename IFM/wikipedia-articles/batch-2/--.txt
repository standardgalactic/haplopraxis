   From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
   "Double dash" redirects here. For the video game, see [59]Mario Kart:
   Double Dash.

   --, a string of two [60]hyphen-minus characters, may approximate or
   refer to:
     * [61]En dash (–)
     * [62]Em dash (—), as a typewriter approximation
     * [63]Sig dashes (--), the email and Usenet signature delimiter
     * For [64]Unix commands, used as a prefix for command options. By
       itself, it typically means the end of command options.
     * The [65]decrement operator in some programming languages
     * [66]Inline comments in some programming languages
