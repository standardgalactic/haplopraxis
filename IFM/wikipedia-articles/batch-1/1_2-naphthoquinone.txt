   Chemical compound

   1,2-Naphthoquinone or ortho-naphthoquinone is a [95]polycyclic
   [96]aromatic [97]organic compound with formula C^
   [10]H^
   [6]O^
   [2]. This yellow solid is prepared by oxidation of
   1-amino-2-hydroxynaphthalene with [98]ferric chloride.^[99][1]

Occurrence

   [[100]edit]

   This [101]diketone (an ortho-[102]quinone) is a metabolite of
   [103]naphthalene. It arises from the
   [104]naphthalene-1,2-oxide.^[105][2]

   It is also found in diesel exhaust particles. The accumulation of this
   toxic metabolite in rats from doses of naphthalene has been shown to
   cause eye damage, including the formation of [106]cataracts.^[107][3]
