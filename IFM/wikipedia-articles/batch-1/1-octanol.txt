   Chemical compound

   1-Octanol, also known as octan-1-ol, is the [146]organic compound with
   the [147]molecular formula CH[3](CH[2])[7]OH. It is a [148]fatty
   alcohol. Many other [149]isomers are also known generically as
   [150]octanols. 1-Octanol is manufactured for the synthesis of esters
   for use in [151]perfumes and [152]flavorings. It has a pungent odor.
   [153]Esters of octanol, such as [154]octyl acetate, occur as components
   of [155]essential oils.^[156][3] It is used to evaluate the
   lipophilicity of pharmaceutical products.

Preparation

   [[157]edit]

   Octanol is mainly produced industrially by the oligomerization of
   [158]ethylene using [159]triethylaluminium followed by oxidation of the
   [160]alkylaluminium products. This route is known as the [161]Ziegler
   alcohol synthesis.^[162][3] An idealized synthesis is shown:

          Al(C[2]H[5])[3] + 9 C[2]H[4] → Al(C[8]H[17])[3]
          Al(C[8]H[17])[3] + 3 O + 3 H[2]O → 3 HOC[8]H[17] + Al(OH)[3]

   The process generates a range of alcohols, which can be separated by
   [163]distillation.

   The Kuraray process defines an alternative route to 1-octanol, but
   using C4 + C4 building strategy. [164]1,3-Butadiene is dimerized
   concomitant with the addition of one molecule of water. This conversion
   is catalyzed by palladium complexes. The resulting doubly unsaturated
   alcohol is then hydrogenated.^[165][4]

Water/octanol partitioning

   [[166]edit]

   Main article: [167]Octanol-water partition coefficient

   between water and octanol is used to calculate the [169]partition
   coefficient, P, of that molecule (often expressed as its logarithm to
   the base 10, log P). Water/octanol partitioning is a relatively good
   approximation of the partitioning between the [170]cytosol and
   [171]lipid [172]membranes of living systems.^[173][5]

   partition coefficient to be well approximated by a function of the

Properties and uses

   [[177]edit]

   With a flash point of 81 °C, 1-octanol is not seriously flammable,
   though its [178]autoignition temperature is as low as 245 °C. 1-Octanol
   is mainly consumed as a precursor to perfumes.^[179][3] It has been
   examined for controlling [180]essential tremor and other types of
   involuntary neurological tremors because evidence indicates it can
   relieve tremor symptoms at lower doses than are required to obtain a
   similar level of symptomatic relief from consumption of ethanol,
   thereby reducing the risk alcohol intoxication at therapeutic
   dosages.^[181][8]

   1-Octanol [182]hydrogen bonds to [183]Lewis bases. It is a [184]Lewis
   acid in the [185]ECW model and its acid parameters are E[A] = 0.85 and
   C [A] = 0.87.^[186][9]
