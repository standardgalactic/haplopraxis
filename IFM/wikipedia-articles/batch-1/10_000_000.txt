   10,000,000 (ten million) is the [109]natural number following 9,999,999
   and preceding 10,000,001.

   In [110]scientific notation, it is written as 10^7.

   In [111]South Asia except for [112]Sri Lanka, it is known as the
   [113]crore.

   In [114]Cyrillic numerals, it is known as the vran (вран — [115]raven).
