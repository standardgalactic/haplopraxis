   From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
   (Redirected from [57]-196˚C Strong Zero)
   Japanese alcoholic beverage

   −196 °C Strong Zero (commonly referred to as Strong Zero or −196) is a
   brand of [58]chūhai produced by [59]Suntory Global Spirits.^[60][1] It
   is currently sold in [61]Japan, [62]Taiwan, the [63]Philippines,
   [64]Australia,^[65][1] the [66]United States,^[67][2] and the
   [68]United Kingdom.^[69][3] As with other types of chūhai, it is
   available in a variety of different flavors, and is often sold at
   [70]convenience stores and through [71]vending machines.^[72][4] The
   "strong" portion of the name comes from the fact that the drinks have
   relatively high alcohol content (9% [73]ABV in Japan).^[74][5] The
   "zero" refers to what the manufacturer calls "sugar-free".^[75][6]
   According to nutrition labelling standards in Japan, a beverage product
   can be labelled as sugar-free as long as it does not exceed 0.5 g of
   sugar per 100 mL.^[76][7]

   Strong Zero is made by freezing fruit in [77]liquid nitrogen. Liquid
   nitrogen has a boiling point of −195.8 °C (−320 °F; 77 K), and hence
   the "−196 °C" in the name. The frozen whole fruit is then pulverized
   and soaked in [78]beverage alcohol. The final beverage contains the
   fruit juice, [79]shochu, [80]vodka and soda.^[81][8]^[82][6]^[83][1]
   The process produces a relatively low calorie and low sugar drink. As
   with many food and drink products available in Japan, new flavors are
   often released to coincide with the seasonality of different
   ingredients.^[84][9]^[85][10]

Australia

   [[86]edit]

   The brand was launched in Australia in June 2021 under the name "−196",
   initially with a "Double Lemon" flavour packaged in 330 mL
   cans.^[87][1] The Double Lemon product has 6% ABV, 0.3 g sugar, and 122
   calories per serving.^[88][11] The launch of −196 Double Lemon in
   Australia in 2021 was the largest launch in the Australian
   [89]ready-to-drink (RTD) alcoholic beverage market in the preceding
   five years, and the product initially went out of stock upon its
   introduction.^[90][12]^[91][13] The initial launch was supported by two
   ads featuring Japanese characters of a sumo wrestler and a samurai
   warrior that play with a lemon to create the lemon juice for the
   premix. The ad campaign was awarded with three Australian Effie Awards
   in 2022.^[92][14]

   Following the success of the Double Lemon flavour, Beam Suntory
   launched a "Double Grape" flavour in Australia in October
   2022.^[93][15] The Double Grape flavour comes in the same size cans and
   has the same ABV, and has 122 calories and has 0.0 g of sugar per
   serve.^[94][11]

United States

   [[95]edit]

   The brand launched "-196", pronounced "Minus One Nine Six",^[96][16] in
   the United States in September 2024. The initial flavors offered are
   "Lemon", "Peach", and "Grapefruit".
