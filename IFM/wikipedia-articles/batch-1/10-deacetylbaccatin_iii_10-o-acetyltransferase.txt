   In [83]enzymology, a 10-deacetylbaccatin III 10-O-acetyltransferase
   ([84]EC [85]2.3.1.167) is an [86]enzyme that [87]catalyzes the
   [88]chemical reaction

          acetyl-CoA + 10-deacetylbaccatin III
          [MATH: <semantics> <mrow class="MJX-TeXAtom-ORD"> <mstyle
          displaystyle="true" scriptlevel="0"> <mo class="MJX-variant"
          stretchy="false">⇌</mo> </mstyle> </mrow> <annotation
          encoding="application/x-tex">{\displaystyle \rightleftharpoons
          }</annotation> </semantics> :MATH]
          {\displaystyle \rightleftharpoons } CoA + baccatin III

   Thus, the two [89]substrates of this enzyme are [90]acetyl-CoA and
   [91]10-deacetylbaccatin III, whereas its two [92]products are [93]CoA
   and [94]baccatin III.

   This enzyme belongs to the family of [95]transferases, specifically
   those [96]acyltransferases transferring groups other than aminoacyl
   groups. The [97]systematic name of this enzyme class is
   acetyl-CoA:taxan-10beta-ol O-acetyltransferase. This enzyme
   participates in [98]diterpenoid biosynthesis.
