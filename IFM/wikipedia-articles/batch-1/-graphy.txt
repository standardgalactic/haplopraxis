   From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
   English language suffix
   "graphy" redirects here. Not to be confused with [65]Graph
   (disambiguation).
   Look up [66]-graphy in Wiktionary, the free dictionary.

   The English [67]suffix -graphy means a "field of study" or related to
   "writing" a book, and is an anglicization of the French -graphie
   inherited from the Latin -graphia, which is a transliterated direct
   borrowing from Greek.

Arts

   [[68]edit]
     * [69]Cartography – art and field of making maps
     * [70]Choreography – art of creating and arranging dances or ballets
     * [71]Cinematography – art of making lighting and camera choices when
       recording photographic images for the cinema.
     * [72]Collagraphy - In [73]printmaking, a fine art technique in which
       [74]collage materials are used as ink-carrying imagery on a
       printing plate.
     * [75]Iconography – art of interpreting the content by icons
     * [76]Klecksography – art of making images from inkblots
     * [77]Lithography – [78]planographic printing technique
     * [79]Photography – art, practice or occupation of taking and
       printing photographs
     * [80]Photolithography – method for microfabrication in electronics
       manufacturing
     * [81]Pornography – practice, occupation and result of producing
       sexually arousing imagery or words
     * [82]Pyrography – art of decorating wood or other materials with
       burn marks
     * [83]Serigraphy – [84]printmaking technique that uses a stencil made
       of fine synthetic material through which ink is forced
     * [85]Tasseography – art of reading tea leaves
     * [86]Thermography – thermal imaging
     * [87]Tomography – three-dimensional imaging
     * [88]Typography – art and techniques of [89]type design
     * [90]Videography – art and techniques of filming video
     * [91]Vitreography – in [92]printmaking, a fine art technique that
       uses glass printing matrices
     * [93]Xerography – means of copying documents

Writing

   [[94]edit]
     * [95]Cacography – bad handwriting or spelling
     * [96]Calligraphy – art of fine handwriting
     * [97]Orthography – rules of correct writing
     * [98]Palaeography – study of historical handwriting
     * [99]Pictography – use of pictographs
     * [100]Steganography – art of writing hidden messages
     * [101]Stenography – art of writing in shorthand

Types of works

   [[102]edit]
   See also: [103]Bibliography § Non-book_material
     * [104]Bibliography – list of [105]writings, typically those used or
       considered by an author in preparing a particular work or research
          + [106]Metabibliography – bibliography of bibliographies
     * [107]Biography – account of a person's life
          + [108]Autobiography – biography of a person written by
            themselves
     * [109]Discography – list of recorded music, or other [110]sound
       recordings/auditory media
     * [111]Filmography – list of [112]films, [113]documentaries, or other
       visual media
     * [114]Ludography (or gameography) – list of [115]games, specifically
       [116]video games
     * Webography (or webliography or arachniography) – list of
       [117]websites, or [118]URLs

Fields of study

   [[119]edit]
     * [120]Areography – geography of Mars (studies the physical features
       of the planet)
     * [121]Cartography – study and making of maps
     * [122]Cosmography – study and making of maps of the universe or
       cosmos
     * [123]Cryptography – study of securing information
     * [124]Crystallography – study of crystals
     * [125]Demography – study of the characteristics of human
       populations, such as size, growth, density, distribution, and vital
       statistics
     * [126]Encephalography – recording of voltages from the brain
     * [127]Ethnography – study of cultures
     * [128]Floriography – language of flowers
     * [129]Geography – study of the lands, features, inhabitants, and
       phenomena of the [130]Earth.
          + [131]Physiography – study of the processes and patterns in the
            Earth's environment.
          + [132]Anthropogeography – study of human society's interactions
            and relationships with the environment.
     * [133]Hagiography – study of saints
     * [134]Historiography – study of the methods of historians
     * [135]Holography – study and mapping of computer project imaged
       called Holograms for interactive and assisted computations.
     * [136]Hydrography – measurement and description of any waters
     * [137]Oceanography – exploration and scientific study of the ocean
       and its phenomena
     * [138]Orography – science and study of mountains
     * [139]Radiography – use of X-rays to produce medical images
     * [140]Reprography – reproduction of graphics through mechanical or
       electrical means
     * [141]Selenography – study and mapping of the physical features of
       the Moon
     * [142]Topography – study of Earth's surface shape and features or
       those of planets, moons and asteroids
     * [143]Uranography – study and mapping of stars and space objects
