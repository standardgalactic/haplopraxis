   Internet country-code top level domain for Armenia
   This article is about the Internet domain. For the file extension, see
   [158]Automake.

   CAPTION: .am

   [159][220px-Logo-am-official-isoc.svg.png]
   Introduced 26 August 1994
   [160]TLD type [161]Country code top-level domain
   Status Active
   [162]Registry [163]AMNIC
   Sponsor [164]ISOC-AM (local chapter of [165]Internet Society)
   Intended use Entities connected with  [166]Armenia
   Actual use Primarily in Armenia and [167]Artsakh; some [168]domain hack
   use elsewhere, e.g. Instagr.am, and some use by [169]AM radio stations
   Registration restrictions Some generic names reserved; registry
   reserves right to revoke registrations due to spam, obscenity, or other
   illegal or immoral activity
   Structure Registrations permitted directly at second level
   Documents [170]AM TLD policy
   Dispute policies Not available
   [171]DNSSEC yes
   Registry website [172]AMNIC

   .am is the [173]internet [174]country code [175]top-level domain
   ([176]ccTLD) for [177]Armenia. The Armenia Network Information Centre
   is managed by the Internet Society of Armenia and is headquartered in
   [178]Yerevan.

Regulation

   [[179]edit]

   The registry for .am is operated by ISOC-AM,^[180][1] the local chapter
   of the [181]Internet Society.

   Regulatory notes:
     * Any person in the world can register a .am, .com.am, .net.am,
       .org.am domain for a fee.
     * Each domain name is subject to review. Generally, each review takes
       about 2 or 3 working days.
     * [182]Armenian law prohibits its [183]domain names from being used
       for [184]spam, [185]pornography, or [186]terrorism sites.^[187][2]
     * The AM-NIC was moved over to [188]IPv6 address compatibility in
       line with the global [189]DNS.
     * [190]Unicode compatible names will not be instituted at AM-NIC
       until all issues related to [191]IPv6 are resolved.

Usage in domain hacks

   [[192]edit]

   Domains within the .am ccTLD enjoy popularity due to the connection to
   [193]AM radio (similar to the [194].fm and [195].tv ccTLDs), and the
   [196]ability to form [197]English words ending in "am" — e.g. the
   mobile photo sharing service [198]Instagram uses the Armenian domain
   name Instagr.am. The live video streaming service Stre.am uses the TLD
   to form their operative keyword, "Stream". American music artist and
   producer [199]will.i.am uses the domain for his website. Such
   unconventional usage of [200]TLDs in domain names are called
   [201]domain hacks.

Second top-level domain

   [[202]edit]
   Main article: [203].հայ

   In 2014, a new Armenian top-level domain name was added, intended for
   [204]Armenian language domain names. The TLD is .հայ (“hy”), from the
   [205]ethnonym for Armenian. It launched in 2016.

Partnerships

   [[206]edit]

   Armenia is a member of the [207]Council of European National Top Level
   Domain Registries and [208]RIPE NCC.
