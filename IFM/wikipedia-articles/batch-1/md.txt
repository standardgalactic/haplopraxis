   Internet country code top-level domain for Moldova
   This article is about an Internet domain. For the file suffix, see
   [145]Markdown.

   CAPTION: .md

   [146][220px-Domain_.md_logo.png]
   Introduced 24 March 1994
   [147]TLD type [148]Country code top-level domain
   Status Active
   [149]Registry STISC^[150][1]
   Intended use Entities connected with [151]Moldova
   Actual use Very popular in Moldova, limited use by [152]medical
   doctors,^[153][2] limited use by [154]Markdown-related
   applications^[155][3]^[156][4]
   Registered domains over 25,000 (2020-07-25)^[157][5]
   Registration restrictions Prohibited domain names with bad taste, foul
   language, injurious to public order or to public sensibilities, with
   offending character, or with obscene or pornographic words
   Structure Registrations are taken directly at the second level
   Documents [158]Terms and conditions
   Dispute policies [159]UDRP
   [160]DNSSEC yes
   Registry website [161]nic.md

   .md is the [162]Internet [163]country code [164]top-level domain
   ([165]ccTLD) for [166]Moldova introduced on March 24, 1994.

   The price of domain registration is 450 [167]lei^[168][5] or US$39
   (including [169]VAT)^[170][6] per year.

   The TLD is currently administered by STISC (Service for Information
   Technology and Cyber Security);^[171][1] before being merged into
   STISC, the Moldovan state company MoldData had an exclusive agreement
   to administer and distribute domain names within the .MD
   domain.^[172][7]

Second-level domain names

   [[173]edit]

   The official regulations list a number of second-level domain names for
   official state and local government use, as well as a list of generic
   second-level domain names:^[174][8]
     * .com.md – [175]Commercial entities
     *

   .srl.md – [176]Private limited companies

     .sa.md – [177]Publicly traded companies



   .net.md – Companies which offer Internet services

     .org.md – Other organizations, including [178]NGOs and not-for-profit
   organizations

     .acad.md – Academic and educational institutions

   .com.md, .org.md, .info.md, and .pro.md [179]third-level domain names
   can be registered via MoldData for US$12.^[180][6]

History

   [[181]edit]

   The establishment of the .md TLD was approved in March 1994 by the
   University of Southern California's [182]Information Sciences Institute
   ([183]IANA's predecessor). The first Moldovan Internet server,
   mdearn.md, became operational on January 1, 1994. In May 1995, .md was
   fully delegated to RCI (the Moldovan Republican Centre for Informatics,
   established September 1, 1993^[184][9]).

   In 1998, RCI entered into a contractual agreement with [185]Domain Name
   Trust, a US-based company, granting it the rights to operate and
   oversee the TLD. Domain Name Trust then proceeded to sell its rights to
   operate .md to DotMD, a US-based LLC, which went bankrupt in October
   2002. During the resulting bankruptcy court proceedings, authority over
   .md was ordered to be returned to the state of Moldova in February
   2003.^[186][10]^[187][2]

   RCI was renamed into MoldData on 12 July 2001, formalizing its
   transformation and succession on 19 September.^[188][11] On 30 August
   2019, by order of the Moldovan government, MoldData was absorbed into
   STISC (Service for Information Technology and Cyber Security), which
   took over the responsibility of the national registrar for the .md
   TLD.^[189][12]^[190][1]

   In November 2007, the number of domains registered under the .md TLD
   reached 12,000.^[191][9]
