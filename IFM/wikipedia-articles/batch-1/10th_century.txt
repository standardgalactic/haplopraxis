   [222][300px-East-Hem_900ad.jpg] Eastern Hemisphere at the beginning of
   the 10th century

   The 10th century was the period from [223]901 (represented by the
   [224]Roman numerals CMI) through [225]1000 (M) in accordance with the
   [226]Julian calendar, and the last century of the [227]1st millennium.

   In China, the [228]Song dynasty was established, with most of China
   reuniting after the fall of the [229]Tang dynasty and the following
   [230]Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms period. The [231]Muslim World
   experienced a [232]cultural zenith, especially in [233]al-Andalus under
   the [234]Caliphate of Córdoba and in the [235]Samanid Empire under
   [236]Ismail Samani. The [237]Abbasid Caliphate continued to exist but
   with reduced central authority. Additionally, there was a cultural
   flourishing for the [238]Byzantine Empire, which also reconquered some
   lost territories, and the [239]First Bulgarian Empire, as well as the
   [240]Holy Roman Empire during the [241]Ottonian Renaissance. The
   historian [242]Lynn White mentions of the period that "to the modern
   eye, it is very nearly the darkest of the [243]Dark Ages ... if it was
   dark, it was the darkness of the womb".^[244][1] [245]Caesar Baronius
   famously described it as the Iron Century, because it was 'iron in its
   harshness and in its sterility of goodness', while [246]Lorenzo Valla
   gave it the similar name "Age of Lead and Iron".^[247][2]^: 2 

Events

   [[248]edit]
     * c. [249]950: The beginning of the [250]Medieval Warm
       Period.^[251][3]

Africa

   [[252]edit]
     * The Christian [253]Nubian kingdom reaches its peak of prosperity
       and military power (see [254]Early history of Sudan).
     * c. [255]909: The [256]Fatimid Caliphate arises in [257]Kabylie,
       [258]Kutama Tribe.
     * c. [259]948: The [260]Nri Kingdom in what is now Southeastern
       [261]Nigeria starts.
     * [262]969: Cairo is founded near Fustat by the [263]Fatimid dynasty.
     * c. [264]980: [265]Al-Azhar University is established in [266]Cairo
       by the [267]Fatimid dynasty.
     * c. [268]980: Queen [269]Gudit leads her rebellion against the
       Aksumite dynasty of Abyssinia.

Americas

   [[270]edit]
     * Collapse of the central lowland [271]Maya civilization.
       Post-Classic Maya period begins. [272]Chichen Itza becomes a
       regional capital on the [273]Yucatán Peninsula
     * Rise of the [274]Toltecs in [275]Mexico
     * Golden age of the [276]Ancestral Puebloans ([277]Pueblo II Era)
     * The [278]Mississippian culture begins in present-day [279]Southern
       United States
     * [280]987: Ah Mekat Tutul Xiu unified [281]Uxmal, [282]Mayapan, and
       [283]Chichen Itza founding The [284]League of Mayapan.

Asia

   [[285]edit]
   [286][200px-Yogini_Goddess_from_Tamil_Nadu.jpg] This statue of a
   [287]yogini goddess was created in Kaveripakkam in [288]Tamil Nadu,
   [289]India, during the 10th century.
     * [290]Buddhist temple construction commences at [291]Bagan,
       [292]Burma
     * Seafarers and merchants from [293]Champa had contacts with Brunei
       and [294]Ma-i.^[295][4]
     * [296]907: [297]Zhu Quanzhong deposes [298]Emperor Ai of Tang and
       establishes a new [299]Later Liang dynasty.
     * 907: [300]Sumbing volcano erupts, according to Rukam
       inscription.[301] [200px-Five_Dynasties_Ten_Kingdoms_923_CE.png]
       China in 923, during the [302]Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms
       period
     * 907: King [303]Balitung creates the [304]Mantyasih inscription
       containing the list of [305]Mataram kings, moves the capital from
       Mamratipura to Poh Pitu, and expands Prambanan temple.
     * [306]910: [307]Parantaka I of the [308]Chola dynasty drives out the
       [309]Pandyan from southern [310]India into Lanka (now [311]Sri
       Lanka), which he also eventually conquers.
     * [312]914: The [313]Warmadewa dynasty rules [314]Bali.^[315][5]
     * [316]919: the first use of [317]gunpowder in battle occurs with the
       Chinese [318]Battle of Langshan Jiang (Wolf Mountain River), where
       the [319]Wuyue naval fleet under [320]Qian Yuanguan defeats the
       [321]Wu fleet. Qian had used [322]flamethrowers ignited by
       gunpowder fuses to burn the Wu fleet.
     * [323]928: [324]Ziyarid dynasty is established in northern Iran.
     * 928: During the reign of [325]King Wawa, the capital of
       [326]Mataram Kingdom in [327]Kewu Plain is devastated, probably by
       the massive eruption of [328]Mount Merapi.
     * [329]929: [330]Mpu Sindok moves the seat of power of the Mataram
       Kingdom from Kewu Plain in [331]Central Java to Tamwlang in
       [332]East Java and establishes [333]Isyana dynasty. The shift is
       probably as a result of the eruption of Mount Merapi and/or
       invasion from [334]Srivijaya.^[335][6]
     * [336]930s: [337]Persian [338]Shia [339]Buyid dynasty establishes
       and controls central and western part of [340]Iran as well as most
       of [341]Iraq.
     * [342]936: [343]Goryeo dynasty unifies [344]Later Three Kingdoms of
       Korea.
     * [345]937: [346]Mpu Sindok moves the capital again from Tamwlang to
       Watugaluh, both near bank of [347]Brantas River in modern
       [348]Jombang in [349]East Java.
     * [350]960: [351]Zhao Kuangyin establishes [352]Song dynasty.
     * 960: [353]Seljuks convert to Islam.
     * [354]965: [355]Khazar kingdom is attacked and defeated by
       [356]Kievan Rus.
     * [357]971: [358]Song dynasty recorded the ancient sovereign state of
       [359]Ma-i on the list of states conducting trade in the south seas
       and the government's efforts to regulate and tax this "luxurious"
       trade.
     * [360]975: [361]Ghaznavids dynasty, as the first [362]Turk
       [363]Sultanate, was established in [364]Central Asia.
     * [365]979: Song dynasty reunites China.
     * [366]980: Dynastic marriage between princess [367]Mahendradatta of
       Javanese [368]Isyanas and king [369]Udayana of Balinese
       [370]Warmadewas.
     * Coastal cities on the [371]Malay Peninsula are the seed for the
       first recorded [372]Malay [373]kingdoms.
     * [374]990: King [375]Dharmawangsa of Mataram kingdom launches a
       naval invasion on [376]Palembang in an unsuccessful attempt to
       conquer Srivijaya. (to 1006)
     * 990: [377]Airlangga, son of King Udayana and Queen Mahendradatta
       was born in Bali.
     * [378]996: Dharmawangsa commissioned the translation of the
       [379]Mahabharata into [380]Old Javanese.^[381][7]
     * [382]999: [383]Samanid dynasty was defeated and conquered by
       [384]Ghaznavids.

Europe

   [[385]edit]
   [386][260px-Bulgarians_defeat_the_Byzantines_at_Anchialos.jpg] The
   Bulgarian victory at [387]Anchialos.
     * [388]Viking groups settle in northern France.
     * [389]907: Three German armies led by [390]Luitpold, Margrave of
       Bavaria, which entered the Hungarian territory in order to expel
       the Hungarians from the Carpathian Basin, is annihilated by the
       Hungarian army at the [391]Battle of Pressburg.
     * Foundation of [392]Cluny, first federated [393]monastic order.
     * Emperor [394]Simeon the Great solidifies the [395]First Bulgarian
       Empire as one of the most powerful states in Europe.
     * Incursions of [396]Magyar (Hungarian) cavalry throughout Western
       Europe (47 expeditions in [397]Germany, [398]Italy and [399]France,
       [400]899–[401]970).
     * Collapse of [402]Great Moravia.
     * [403]Lions become extinct in Europe by this date, with the last
       dying in [404]Caucasus.
     * [405]907: Loire Vikings overrun [406]Brittany; Breton court flees
       to the [407]England of [408]Edward the Elder.
     * [409]911: The Viking [410]Rollo granted [411]County of Rouen by
       [412]France: official foundation of [413]Normandy.
     * [414]917: (20 August 917) the [415]Bulgarians destroyed the
       Byzantine army in the [416]Battle of Anchialus, one of the
       bloodiest battles in the Middle Ages.
     * [417]927: official recognition of the first independent national
       Church in Europe, the [418]Bulgarian Patriarchate.[419]
       [180px-British_Isles_10th_century.svg.png] Political divisions
       within England, 10th century.
     * 927: [420]Kingdom of England becomes a unified state.
     * c. [421]936: [422]Gorm the Old becomes the first recognized king of
       [423]Denmark, and thus the Danish Monarchy is founded.
     * 936: [424]Alan II, with support from [425]Æthelstan, commences the
       reconquest of [426]Brittany.
     * [427]955: [428]The Battle of Lechfeld (10 August 955) sees a
       decisive victory for [429]Otto I the Great, King of the Germans,
       over the Hungarian [430]harka [431]Bulcsú and the chieftains
       [432]Lél (Lehel) and [433]Súr.
     * [434]966: [435]Mieszko I, first duke of [436]Poland, baptised a
       Christian.
     * [437]968: [438]Battle of Silistra between Kievan Rus and Bulgars.
     * [439]985–[440]986: The [441]Norseman [442]Erik the Red begins the
       colonisation of [443]Greenland.
     * [444]925: The medieval Croatian state becomes a [445]unified
       kingdom under King [446]Tomislav
     * [447]988: [448]Vladimir I, Prince of [449]Kievan Rus', baptised a
       Christian
     * c. [450]1000: Establishment of the [451]Eastern Settlement and
       [452]Western Settlement in [453]Greenland.
     * 1000: [454]Sweyn Forkbeard conquers [455]Norway.
     * Second half of the 10th century: Page with David the Psalmist, from
       the [456]Paris Psalter, is made. It is now kept at
       [457]Bibliothéque Nationale, [458]Paris.
     * Late 10th or early 11th century: The [459]Archangel Michael icon,
       is made. It is now kept at the [460]Treasury of the Cathedral of
       Saint Mark, [461]Venice.

Oceania

   [[462]edit]
     * c. 950: Formation of the [463]Tu'i Tonga Empire and of the
       [464]Tuʻi Tonga dynasty in [465]Tonga.

Inventions, discoveries, introductions

   [[466]edit]
   Main article: [467]Timeline of historic inventions § 10th century
   [468][200px-FireLanceAndGrenade10thCenturyDunhuang.jpg] Earliest known
   representation of a gun (a [469]fire lance) and a [470]grenade (upper
   right), from the cave murals of [471]Dunhuang, China, 10th century.
     * 10th century–12th century: Seated Guaryin [472]Bodhisattva, is made
       during the [473]Liao dynasty. It is now kept at The
       [474]Nelson-Atkins Museum of Art, [475]Kansas City, Missouri.
     * Construction begins on the [476]Brihadeeswarar Temple of
       [477]India, during the reign of [478]Rajaraja Chola I.
     * The first [479]pound lock is invented by the Chinese engineer
       [480]Qiao Weiyo, improving the [481]canal lock system.
     * [482]Fire arrows are invented by the Chinese.
     * Earliest known occurrence in [483]Mexico of [484]Lost-wax casting.
     * Three of the [485]Four Great Books of Song are published (the last
       one in [486]1013), which were enormous Chinese [487]encyclopedias
       having millions of written [488]Chinese characters each.
     * [489]Hops first mentioned in connection with [490]beer brewing.
     * [491]Zhang Sixun of [492]China uses for the first time liquid
       [493]mercury (element) instead of water to power the
       [494]escapement mechanism rotating an [495]armillary sphere, since
       liquid mercury does not freeze easily like water during winter, and
       does not rust metal parts.
