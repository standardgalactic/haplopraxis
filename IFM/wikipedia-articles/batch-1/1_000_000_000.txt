   1,000,000,000 (one [188]billion, [189]short scale; one thousand million
   or one milliard, one yard,^[190][1] [191]long scale) is the
   [192]natural number following [193]999,999,999 and preceding
   1,000,000,001. With a number, "billion" can be abbreviated as b,
   bil^[194][2] or bn.^[195][3]^[196][4]

   In standard form, it is written as 1 × 10^9. The [197]metric prefix
   [198]giga indicates 1,000,000,000 times the base unit. Its symbol is
   [199]G.

   One billion years may be called an [200]eon in astronomy or geology.

   Previously in [201]British English (but not in [202]American English),
   the word "billion" referred exclusively to a [203]million millions
   (1,000,000,000,000). However, this is not common anymore, and the word
   has been used to mean one thousand million (1,000,000,000) for several
   decades.^[204][5]

   The term milliard could also be used to refer to 1,000,000,000; whereas
   "milliard" is rarely used in English,^[205][6] variations on this name
   often appear in [206]other languages.

   In the [207]Indian numbering system, it is known as 100 [208]crore or 1
   [209]arab.

   1,000,000,000 is also the [210]cube of [211]1000.
   [212][400px-Visualisation_1_billion.svg.png] Visualization of powers of
   ten from one to 1 billion

Sense of scale

   [[213]edit]

   The facts below give a sense of how large 1,000,000,000 (10^9) is in
   the context of time according to current scientific evidence:

Time

   [[214]edit]

     * 10^9 seconds (1 gigasecond) equal 11,574 days, 1 hour, 46 minutes
       and 40 seconds (approximately 31.7 years, or 31 years, 8 months, 8
       days).
     * About 10^9 minutes ago, the [215]Roman Empire was flourishing and
       Christianity was emerging. (10^9 minutes is roughly 1,901 years.)
     * About 10^9 hours ago, modern human beings and their ancestors were
       living in the [216]Stone Age (more precisely, the [217]Middle
       Paleolithic). (10^9 hours is roughly 114,080 years.)
     * About 10^9 days ago, [218]Australopithecus, an ape-like creature
       related to an ancestor of modern humans, roamed the African
       [219]savannas. (10^9 days is roughly 2.738 million years.)
     * About 10^9 months ago, [220]dinosaurs walked the Earth during the
       late [221]Cretaceous. (10^9 months is roughly 83.3 million years.)
     * About 10^9 years—a [222]gigaannus—ago, the first [223]multicellular
       [224]eukaryotes appeared on Earth.
     * About 10^9 decades ago, the [225]thin disk of the [226]Milky Way
       started to form. (10^9 decades is exactly 10 billion years.)
     * The [227]universe is thought to be about [228]13.8 × 10^9 years
       old.^[229][7]

Distance

   [[230]edit]

     * 10^9 inches is 15,783 miles (25,400 km), more than halfway around
       the world and thus sufficient to reach any point on the globe from
       any other point.
     * 10^9 [231]metres (called a [232]gigametre) is almost three times
       the distance from the [233]Earth to the [234]Moon.
     * 10^9 [235]kilometres (called a [236]terametre) is over six times
       the distance from the [237]Earth to the [238]Sun.

Area

   [[239]edit]

     * A billion square inches could make a square about one half mile on
       a side.
     * A bolt of finely woven [240]1000-TC bed sheet linen with a billion
       thread crossings would have an area of 40 square metres (48 sq yd),
       comparable to the floor area of a motel unit.

Volume

   [[241]edit]

     * There are one billion cubic [242]millimetres in a [243]cubic metre,
       and a billion cubic metres in a [244]cubic kilometre.
     * A billion grains of table salt or granulated sugar would occupy a
       volume of about 2.5 cubic feet (0.071 m^3).
     * A billion cubic inches would be a volume comparable to a large
       commercial building slightly larger than a typical supermarket.

Weight

   [[245]edit]

     * Any object that weighs one billion kilograms (2.2×10^9 lb) would
       weigh about as much as 5,525 empty [246]Boeing 747-400s.
     * A cube of iron that weighs one billion pounds (450,000,000 kg)
       would be 38.62 metres (126.7 ft) on each side.

Products

   [[247]edit]

     * As of July 2016, [248]Apple has sold one billion
       [249]iPhones.^[250][8] This makes the iPhone one of the most
       successful product lines in history, surpassing the
       [251]PlayStation and the [252]Rubik's Cube.
     * As of January 2023, [253]Facebook has 2.963 billion users.^[254][9]

Nature

   [[255]edit]

     * A small mountain, slightly larger than [256]Stone Mountain in
       Georgia, United States, would weigh (have a mass of) a billion
       tons.
     * There are billions of worker ants in the largest ant colony in the
       world,^[257][10] which covers almost 4,000 miles (6,400 km) of the
       Mediterranean coast.
     * In 1804, the [258]world population was one billion.

Count

   [[259]edit]

   A is a cube; B consists of 1000 cubes the size of cube A, C consists of
   1000 cubes the size of cube B; and D consists of 1000 cubes the size of
   cube C. Thus there are 1 million A-sized cubes in C; and 1,000,000,000
   A-sized cubes in D.
