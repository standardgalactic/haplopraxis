Mathematics, science and technology

   [[55]edit]
     * 0th or zeroth, an ordinal for the number [56]0
     * [57]0th dimension, a topological space
     * [58]0th element, of a data structure in computer science
     * [59]0th law of Thermodynamics
     * [60]Zeroth (software), deep learning software for mobile devices

Other uses

   [[61]edit]
     * [62]0th grade, another name for kindergarten
     * [63]January 0 or January 0th, an alternate name for December 31
     * [64]0 Avenue, a road in British Columbia straddling the Canada-US
       border
