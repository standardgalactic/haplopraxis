   Internet top-level domain

   CAPTION: .scot

   [80][220px-DotScot_Registry_Logo.svg.png]
   Introduced Pioneer phase launched on 15 July 2014. Delegated to the
   root in June 2014; First proposed in 2000.^[81][1]
   [82]TLD type [83]GeoTLD
   Status General registrations beginning on 23 September 2014,
   registrations open for interest and trademark holders from 15 July
   2014.
   Intended use  [84]Scotland, [85]Scottish culture, [86]Gaelic and
   [87]Scots languages
   Registration restrictions Requires connection to Scotland or Scottish
   culture.
   [88]DNSSEC Yes
   Registry website [89]dot.scot

   .scot is a [90]GeoTLD for Scotland and [91]Scottish culture, including
   the [92]Gaelic and [93]Scots languages.^[94][2]

   In 2008 [95]dotCYMRU, [96]dotEUS, dotSCOT and [97]dotBZH formed
   [98]ECLID^[[99]clarification needed]. Later it was decided to allow
   newly proposed [100]top-level domains for introduction some time in
   2013, and a list of applications for these was published in June 2012;
   the domain .scot was included.^[101][3]

   On 27 January 2014, dotScot Registry, a not-for-profit organization
   established in 2009, announced that it had agreed terms to operate the
   .scot domain name, with plans to get it up and running later in summer
   of 2014.^[102][4]

   On 15 July 2014, .scot was officially launched.^[103][5] The first
   .scot domain name to go live was calico.scot, registered by hosting
   company Calico Internet Ltd.^[104][5]

   On 17 February 2015, the [105]Scottish Government migrated its website
   from scotland.gov.uk to gov.scot.^[106][6]^[107][7] Likewise, the
   [108]Scottish Parliament moved from scottish.parliament.uk to
   parliament.scot in May 2016,^[109][8] to coincide with the [110]2016
   elections.^[111][9]

   The 2017 Global Amendment to the base New [112]GeoTLD Registry
   Agreement is effective as of 31 July 2017.^[113][10]

   On 3 May 2018 the dotScot Registry lifted registration restrictions on
   locality domains (based on towns, etc.) and other premium
   names.^[114][11]
