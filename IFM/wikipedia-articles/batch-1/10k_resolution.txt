   From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
   Video or display resolutions with a width of around 10,000 pixels

   10K resolution refers to a horizontal [60]display resolutions of
   approximately 10,000 pixels. Unlike [61]4K UHD and [62]8K UHD, there
   are no 10K resolutions defined in the [63]UHDTV broadcast standard. The
   first 10K displays demonstrated were [64]ultrawide "21:9" screens with
   a resolution of 10240 × 4320, the same vertical resolution as 8K UHD.

History

   [[65]edit]

   On June 5, 2015, Chinese manufacturer BOE showed a 10K display with an
   aspect ratio of 64:27 (≈21:9) and a resolution of 10240 × 4320.^[66][1]

   In November 2016, the [67]Consumer Technology Association published
   CTA-861-G, an update to their standard for digital video transmission
   formats. This revision added support for 10240 × 4320, a 10K resolution
   with an aspect ratio of 64:27 (≈21:9), at up to 120 Hz.^[68][2]

   On January 4, 2017, [69]HDMI version 2.1 was officially announced, and
   was later released on November 28, 2017.^[70][3]^[71][4]^[72][5] HDMI
   2.1 includes support for all the formats listed in the CTA-861-G
   standard, including 10K (10240 × 4320) at up to 120 Hz.^[73][4]^[74][5]
   HDMI 2.1 specifies a new Ultra High Speed HDMI cable which supports a
   bandwidth of up to 48 [75]Gbit/s. [76]Display Stream Compression (DSC)
   1.2a is used for video formats higher than 8K resolution with 4:2:0
   [77]chroma subsampling.^[78][4]^[79][5]^[80][6]

   10K resolutions are also sometimes seen in the case of gaming, for
   instance high resolution screenshots in the case of Minecraft with the
   OptiFine mod.^[81][7]

Cameras

   [[82]edit]

   As of 2021^[83][update], there are multiple companies producing photo
   cameras capable of 10K and higher resolutions, such as [84]Phase
   One,^[85][8]^[86][9] [87]Fujifilm,^[88][10]^[89][11]
   [90]Hasselblad,^[91][12]^[92][13] and [93]Sony.^[94][14]^[95][15] Other
   companies also create sensors capable of 10K resolution, though they
   are mostly not available to the general public, and are often used for
   scientific or industrial purposes.^[96][16]^[97][17]^[98][18]

   [99]Blackmagic Design is the only company producing a video camera
   capable of filming in resolutions 10K or higher with their URSA Mini
   Pro 12K.^[100][19]^[101][20]
