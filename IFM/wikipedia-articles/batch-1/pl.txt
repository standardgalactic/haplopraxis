   CAPTION: .pl

   [152][220px-DNS-NASK.svg.png]
   Introduced July 30, 1990; 34 years ago (1990-07-30)
   [153]TLD type [154]Country code
   Status Active
   [155]Registry [156]NASK
   Sponsor NASK
   Intended use Entities connected with [157]Poland
   Actual use Very popular in Poland
   Registered domains 2,467,559 (30 September 2020)^[158][1]
   Registration restrictions No restrictions stated
   Structure Registrations can be made directly at second level, or at
   third level beneath various second level labels
   Dispute policies [159]PIIT arbitration (for Polish entities)
   [160]WIPO expedited arbitration (for foreign entities)
   [161]DNSSEC yes
   Registry website [162]dns.pl

   .pl
   is the [163]Internet [164]country code [165]top-level domain
   ([166]ccTLD) for [167]Poland, administered by [168]NASK, the Polish
   research and development organization. It is one of the founding
   members of [169]CENTR.

History

   [[170]edit]

   The
   .pl domain was created in 1990, following the mitigation of the
   [171]COCOM embargo on technological collaboration with
   post-[172]communist countries. The first [173]subdomain in
   .pl was .pwr.pl, belonging to the [174]Wrocław University of
   Technology.

   In 2008 the number of registered .pl domain names exceeded one million,
   whereas at the end of 2013 the registry comprised more than 2.4 million
   domain names.^[175][2]^[176][1]

   Each domain must be registered in the National Domain Name Registry.

Second-level domains

   [[177]edit]

   Several functional and regional domain endings exist. Most popular are:

Functional domains

   [[178]edit]
     *

   .com.pl,
   .biz.pl,
   .pl: [179]commercial entities

     .net.pl: network infrastructure

     .art.pl: [180]art

     .edu.pl: [181]education

     .gov.pl: [182]government

     .info.pl: (general) information

     .mil.pl: [183]military

     .org.pl: [184]NGOs and not-for-profit organizations

   [[185]edit]

   There are 118 regional domain endings in the

     .krakow.pl: [192]Kraków (city)

     .lublin.pl: [193]Lublin (city)

     .mazowsze.pl: [195]Mazowsze (region)

     .olsztyn.pl: [196]Olsztyn (city)

     .pila.pl: [197]Piła (city)

     .poznan.pl: [198]Poznań (city)

     .rzeszow.pl: [199]Rzeszów (city)

     .szczecin.pl: [200]Szczecin (city)

     .slask.pl: [201]Śląsk (region)

     .torun.pl: [202]Toruń (city)

     .wroc.pl, .wroclaw.pl: [203]Wrocław (city)

     .waw.pl, .warszawa.pl: [204]Warsaw (city)

     .zgora.pl: [205]Zielona Góra (city)
