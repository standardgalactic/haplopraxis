   CAPTION: .as

   [150][220px-DotAS-Bahnschrift_Condensed.svg.png]
   Introduced June 12, 1997
   [151]TLD type [152]Country code top-level domain
   Status Active
   [153]Registry American Samoan Network Information Center
   Sponsor American Samoan Network Information Center
   Intended use Entities connected with [154]American Samoa
   Actual use Used for a variety of purposes, including applications
   unconnected to American Samoa; also used for [155]domain hacks and by
   companies where AS has a special meaning in company names
   Registration restrictions Domains should not be used for pornographic
   or racist material; registry has right of refusal for applications
   Structure Registrations are directly at second level
   Documents [156]Registry policy
   Dispute policies [157]UDRP
   Registry website [158]American Samoan Network Information Center
   [159][220px-Seal_of_American_Samoa.svg.png] Seal of American Samoa

   .as is the [160]Internet [161]country code [162]top-level domain
   ([163]ccTLD) for [164]American Samoa. It is administered by AS Domain
   Registry. Island Networks, which provides registry services for
   [165].gg and [166].je, is also responsible for the technical operations
   of .as.^[167][1]

Usage

   [[168]edit]

   Domain names are free of charge for businesses and individuals resident
   in or associated with American Samoa.

   There is no restriction on registrants, and the domain is also popular
   outside of Samoa. A number of .as names have been registered to and
   used by people, companies and organizations with no connection to
   American Samoa (for example, people and organizations related to
   [169]Asturias, a Spanish region). "AS" or "A/S" is a suffix indicating
   a [170]joint stock company in some countries including [171]Norway,
   [172]Denmark, [173]Estonia and [174]Czech Republic, so this TLD may be
   of use by companies of this sort. Also, some [175]autonomous systems or
   websites providing information about autonomous systems or [176]BGP,
   such as bgp4.as, have registered domain names. It is, sometimes, used
   as a [177]domain hack, since the suffix "-s" means plural in some
   languages like English, Spanish, and Portuguese, thus "-as" would be
   the end of the plural of a word that ends with an "a". Examples of such
   usage include the Brazilian website escol.as, meaning "schools", or
   macc.as/feedback for the public food service feedback form for
   [178]McDonald's in Australia, stemming from the colloquial use of
   'Maccas' to describe the restaurant chain in the country.

2016 legacy registrar vulnerability

   [[179]edit]

   In April 2016, a security blog publicized a vulnerability in a .AS
   legacy registrar system which was claimed might have led to exposure of
   plain-text passwords of domain contacts associated with domains that
   did not have a registrar, and that this could have potentially allowed
   an attacker to make modifications to those domains, or even delete
   them. However, following publication, the AS Domain Registry confirmed
   to the reporter that legacy managed domains were subject to human
   oversight and authentication of all changes and that no attempts had
   been made to take advantage of this apparent vulnerability, and the
   potential exploit was confirmed to have been closed.^[180][2]
