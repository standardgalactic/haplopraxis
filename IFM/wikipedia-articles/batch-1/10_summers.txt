   10 Summers is the debut studio album by American music producer [77]DJ
   Mustard.^[78][1] It was released on August 11, 2014,^[79][2] by
   [80]Pu$haz Ink, [81]Roc Nation and [82]Republic Records. The album,
   [83]produced entirely by DJ Mustard himself, features [84]guest
   appearances from several American rappers, such as [85]Big Sean,
   [86]Fabolous, [87]YG, [88]Rick Ross, [89]Ty Dolla $ign, [90]Lil Wayne,
   [91]2 Chainz, [92]Young Jeezy, [93]Nipsey Hussle, [94]Wiz Khalifa,
   [95]Lil Boosie, [96]Yo Gotti, [97]Iamsu!, [98]TeeFlii, and [99]Dom
   Kennedy, among others.

Release and promotion

   [[100]edit]

   On July 22, 2014, DJ Mustard unveiled the album's track list.^[101][3]
   On August 11, 2014, 10 Summers was released through [102]Google Play
   for free during its first week.
