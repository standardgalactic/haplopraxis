   The [82]enzyme (−)-endo-Fenchol synthase (EC 4.2.3.10) [83]catalyzes
   the [84]chemical reaction

          geranyl diphosphate + H[2]O
          [MATH: <semantics> <mrow class="MJX-TeXAtom-ORD"> <mstyle
          displaystyle="true" scriptlevel="0"> <mo class="MJX-variant"
          stretchy="false">⇌</mo> </mstyle> </mrow> <annotation
          encoding="application/x-tex">{\displaystyle \rightleftharpoons
          }</annotation> </semantics> :MATH]
          {\displaystyle \rightleftharpoons } (−)-endo-fenchol +
          diphosphate

   This enzyme belongs to the family of [85]lyases, specifically those
   carbon-oxygen lyases acting on phosphates. The [86]systematic name of
   this enzyme class is geranyl-diphosphate diphosphate-lyase [cyclizing,
   (−)-endo-fenchol-forming]. Other names in common use include
   (−)-endo-fenchol cyclase, and geranyl pyrophosphate:(−)-endo-fenchol
   cyclase. This enzyme participates in [87]monoterpenoid
   [88]biosynthesis.
