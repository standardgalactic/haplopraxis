   From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

   1.5 (one and one half, three halves, or sesquialterum) may refer to:
     * 1.5 °C is the preferred limit of [51]global warming signed in the
       [52]Paris Agreement
     * 1.5, an album by [53]Big Data
     * [54]Superparticular ratio: 3/2 or 11⁄2
     * [55]Perfect fifth (3/2), a musical interval
     * [56]"1.5" (song), a 2018 song by 21 Savage
