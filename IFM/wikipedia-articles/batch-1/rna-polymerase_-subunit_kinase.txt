   In [82]enzymology, an [RNA-polymerase]-subunit kinase ([83]EC
   [84]2.7.11.23) is an [85]enzyme that [86]catalyzes the [87]chemical
   reaction

          ATP + [DNA-directed RNA polymerase]
          [MATH: <semantics> <mrow class="MJX-TeXAtom-ORD"> <mstyle
          displaystyle="true" scriptlevel="0"> <mo class="MJX-variant"
          stretchy="false">⇌</mo> </mstyle> </mrow> <annotation
          encoding="application/x-tex">{\displaystyle \rightleftharpoons
          }</annotation> </semantics> :MATH]
          {\displaystyle \rightleftharpoons } ADP + phospho-[DNA-directed
          RNA polymerase]

   Thus, the two [88]substrates of this enzyme are [89]ATP and
   [90]DNA-directed RNA polymerase, whereas its two [91]products are
   [92]ADP and phospho-DNA-directed RNA polymerase.

   This enzyme belongs to the family of [93]transferases, specifically
   those transferring a phosphate group to the sidechain [94]oxygen atom
   of [95]serine or [96]threonine residues in [97]proteins
   ([98]protein-serine/threonine kinases). The [99]systematic name of this
   enzyme class is ATP:[DNA-directed RNA polymerase] phosphotransferase.
   Other names in common use include CTD kinase, and STK9.
