   CAPTION: .ai

   [153][220px-DotAI_logo.svg.png]
   Introduced 16 February 1995
   [154]TLD type [155]Country code top-level domain
   Status Active
   [156]Registry [157]Offshore Information Services
   Sponsor [158]Government of Anguilla
   Intended use Entities connected with [159]Anguilla
   Actual use Some use in Anguilla; very popular with companies working in
   the artificial intelligence (AI) industry.
   Registered domains 572,575 (November 2024)^[160][1]
   Registration restrictions None
   Structure Registrations possible at third level, beneath several
   second-level labels, are available to anybody; second-level
   registrations are now available to anybody as well (as of 26 June
   2006).
   Dispute policies [161]UDRP
   [162]DNSSEC Yes
   Registry website [163]nic.com.ai

   .ai is the [164]Internet [165]country code [166]top-level domain
   ([167]ccTLD) for [168]Anguilla, a [169]British Overseas Territory in
   the [170]Caribbean. It is administered by the [171]government of
   Anguilla.

   It is popular with companies and projects related to the
   [172]artificial intelligence industry (AI).^[173][2]^[174][3]^[175][4]

   [176]Google's ad targeting treats .ai as a [177]generic top-level
   domain (gTLD) because "users and website owners frequently see [the
   domain] as being more generic than country-targeted."^[178][5]

Second and third level registrations

   [[179]edit]

   Registrations within off.ai, com.ai, net.ai, and org.ai are available
   worldwide without restriction. From 15 September 2009, second level
   registrations within .ai are available to everyone worldwide.

Registration

   [[180]edit]

   The minimum registration term allowed for a .ai [181]domain is 2 years
   for registration and 2 years for renewal. The authority in charge of
   managing this extension is “WHOIS.AI”. Registrations began on
   1995-02-16. The minimum length is 2 and the maximum is 63 characters.
   There are no requirements for registering a domain, including local and
   foreign residents.

   A .ai domain can be suspended or revoked, if the domain is involved in
   illegal activity such as violating trademarks or copyrights. Usage must
   not violate the laws of Anguilla.^[182][6]

   Anguilla uses the UDRP. Filing a UDRP challenge requires using one of
   the [183]ICANN Approved Dispute Resolution Service Providers. If the
   domain is with an ICANN accredited registrar, they should work with the
   arbitrator. Usually this means either doing nothing or transferring a
   domain. .ai domains are transferable to any desired registrars as the
   registration of domain is done maintaining [184]EPP.^[185][7]

   The character set supported for .ai domain names include A–Z, a–z, 0–9,
   and hyphen. As of November 2022, .ai domains cannot accommodate IDN
   characters.^[186][8] The auction of expired .ai domains is undertaken
   every ten days where expired domains can be procured through the
   auction process.^[187][9]

Valuation

   [[188]edit]

   Domains are $180 for each two-year period. As of December 2017, the
   ".ai" registry supports [189]Extensible Provisioning Protocol.
   Consequently, many registrars are allowed to sell ".ai" domains. Since
   then, the .ai [190]ccTLD has also been popular with [191]artificial
   intelligence companies and organizations. Though such trends are
   primarily seen among new AI based companies or startups, many
   established AI and Tech companies preferred not to opt for .ai domain.
   For example, [192]DeepMind has its domain retained at [193].com;
   [194]Meta has redirected its facebook.ai domain to
   ai.meta.com.^[195][10]

   As of November 2022, expert.ai is the most valued .ai domain that has
   been sold. The sell was brokered and mediated by [196]Sedo in June 2020
   and was finalized at €95,000 (about US$108,000).^[197][11]^[198][12]

Impact on Anguilla's economy

   [[199]edit]

   The registration fees earned from the .ai domains go to the treasury of
   Government of Anguilla. As per a [200]New York Times report, in 2018,
   the total revenue generated out of selling .ai domains was $2.9
   million.^[201][13]^[202][14]

   In 2023, Anguilla’s government made about US$32 million from fees
   collected for registering .ai domains. That amounted to more than 10%
   of [203]gross domestic product for the territory.^[204][15]^[205][16]
