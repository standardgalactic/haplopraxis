   (−)-endo-fenchol dehydrogenase ([77]EC [78]1.1.1.322, l-endo-fenchol
   dehydrogenase, FDH) is an [79]enzyme with [80]systematic name
   (−)-endo-fenchol:NAD(P)^+ oxidoreductase.^[81][1] This enzyme
   [82]catalyses the following [83]chemical reaction

          (−)-endo-fenchol + NAD(P)^+
          [MATH: <semantics> <mrow class="MJX-TeXAtom-ORD"> <mstyle
          displaystyle="true" scriptlevel="0"> <mo class="MJX-variant"
          stretchy="false">⇌</mo> </mstyle> </mrow> <annotation
          encoding="application/x-tex">{\displaystyle \rightleftharpoons
          }</annotation> </semantics> :MATH]
          {\displaystyle \rightleftharpoons } (+)-fenchone + NAD(P)H + H^+

   This enzyme is from the plant [84]Foeniculum vulgare.
