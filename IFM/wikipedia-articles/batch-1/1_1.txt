   From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

   1+1 is a mathematical expression that evaluates to:
     * [69]2 (number) (in ordinary arithmetic)
     * [70]1 (number) (in Boolean algebra with a notation where '+'
       denotes a logical disjunction)
     * [71]0 (number) (in Boolean algebra with a notation where '+'
       denotes 'exclusive or' operation, or in a quotient ring of numbers
       modulo 2)

   The terms 1+1, One Plus One, or One and One may refer to:

1+1

     * [72]1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + ⋯, a mathematical divergent series
     * [73]1+1 (TV channel), a Ukrainian TV channel
     * [74]1+1 (Grin album), 1972
     * [75]1+1 (Herbie Hancock and Wayne Shorter album), 1997
     * [76]"1+1" (song), by Beyoncé Knowles
     * "1+1", a 2021 song by Sia from [77]Music
     * "1+1", a 2021 song by Pentagon from [78]Love or Take

One Plus One

     * [79]OnePlus One, an Android smartphone
     * One Plus One, original title of Jean-Luc Godard's 1968 film
       [80]Sympathy for the Devil
     * One Plus One, 2002 graphic novel [81]published by Oni Press
     * [82]One Plus One (TV programme), a weekly interview show aired by
       ABC in Australia
     * [83]Unomásuno (English: One Plus One), a Mexican newspaper

One and One

     * [84]One and One (musical), an American 1970s off-Broadway musical
       comedy (also known as One & One)
     * [85]One and One (song), written by Billy Steinberg, Rick Nowels and
       Marie-Claire D'Ubaldo and notably covered by Robert Miles feat.
       Maria Nayler
     * "[86]One and One (Ain't I Good Enough)", a 1987 song by Wa Wa Nee
     * One-and-one, a type of [87]free throw in basketball
     * [88]Fish and chips, in Dublin slang

Other

     * [89]1&1 AG, a German telecom company
     * [90]1 and 1 (SHINee album), a 2016 reissue of their album 1 of 1
