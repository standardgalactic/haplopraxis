   Internet country-code top level domain for Mali
   This article is about the top-level domain. For the filename extension,
   see [147]ML (programming language).
   Not to be confused with [148].mil.

   CAPTION: .ml

   [149][220px-.ml_domain.png]
   Introduced 29 September 1993
   [150]TLD type [151]Country code top-level domain
   Status Active
   [152]Registry Agence des Technologies de l’Information et de la
   Communication
   Sponsor Agence des Technologies de l’Information et de la Communication
   Intended use Entities connected with  [153]Mali
   Actual use Sees some use in Mali and for some websites about machine
   learning. Use is relatively rare elsewhere.
   Structure Registrations are at third level beneath various second-level
   names
   Dispute policies [154]UDRP
   Registry website [155]nic.ml

   .ml is the [156]Internet [157]country code [158]top-level domain
   ([159]ccTLD) for [160]Mali.

History

   [[161]edit]

   The domain was initially managed by [162]Sotelma, a Malian
   telecommunications company. After Sotelma was privatised in 2009, the
   .ml zone was redelegated by [163]IANA to the [164]Agence des
   Technologies de l’Information et de la Communication (AGETIC), a Malian
   government agency, and the process completed in 2013.^[165][1] The
   agency then announced that it would give away .ml domains for free in
   partnership with [166]Freenom with a view to improve the usage and the
   knowledge of the IT industry in Mali. It was the first African nation
   to start giving away domains for free.^[167][2]^[168][3]^[169][4] The
   ten-year contract with Freenom expired on 17 July 2023. Since then the
   registry is operated by AGETIC itself and the free domain offer was
   discontinued. All paid Freenom .ml domains were migrated to the new
   system.

Second-level domains

   [[170]edit]

   Registrations are possible at the second level and at the third level
   beneath these names:
     * .com.ml: Companies
     * .net.ml: Internet providers
     * .org.ml: Associations (must show registration); international
       organizations permitted, but must be registered with the local
       administration
     * .edu.ml: Local schools
     * .presse.ml: Local press^[171][5]
     * .gov.ml: Governmental organisations

   Domain names of between one and three characters are considered premium
   domain names with a higher price.

Military emails

   [[172]edit]

   Employees for the [173]United States Armed Forces regularly misspell
   emails—suffixed with the [174].mil TLD—with .ml. In 2013, Dutch
   internet entrepreneur Johannes Zuurbier took on the .ml TLD. He
   attempted to contact the United States government about classified
   information being sent to army.ml and navy.ml in 2014 through Dutch
   diplomats.^[[175]citation needed] The contents of these emails include
   crew and staff lists, maps and photos of installations, naval
   inspection reports, and passwords. Emails that were sent to the .ml TLD
   include the travel itinerary of chief of staff [176]James McConville on
   a trip to [177]Indonesia in 2023, information about [178]Kurdistan
   Workers' Party efforts in the United States, and [179]Australian
   Department of Defence documents detailing issues with Australian
   [180]F-35s. On 17 July 2023, Zuurbier's contract expired and control
   was reverted back to the Malian government.
