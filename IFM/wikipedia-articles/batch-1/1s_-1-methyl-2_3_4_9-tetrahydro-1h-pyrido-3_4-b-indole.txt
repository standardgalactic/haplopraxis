   Chemical compound

   Tetrahydroharman(e), also known as
   1-methyl-1,2,3,4-tetrahydro-β-carboline, is a general name for one of
   two isomers:
    1. (1S)-1-methyl-2,3,4,9-tetrahydro-1H-pyrido[3,4-b]indole
    2. Calligonine
       ((1R)-1-methyl-2,3,4,9-tetrahydro-1H-pyrido[3,4-b]indole)

   Calligonine is a major alkaloid constituent of the roots of Calligonum
   minimum and the bark of [93]Elaeagnus angustifolia. When taken
   internally, it has the effect of substantially lowering blood pressure
   for an extended period of time, similar to [94]reserpine.
