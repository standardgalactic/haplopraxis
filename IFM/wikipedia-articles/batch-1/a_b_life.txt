   [A→B] Life is the debut album by American [72]rock band
   [73]mewithoutYou. It was released on [74]Tooth & Nail Records on June
   18, 2002. The songs "Bullet to Binary" and "Gentlemen" were featured on
   a split album with [75]Norma Jean.
