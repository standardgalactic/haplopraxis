   Internet country-code top level domain for Jersey

   CAPTION: .je

   [139][220px-Channelisles.net_Logo.png]
   Introduced 8 August 1996; 28 years ago (1996-08-08)
   [140]TLD type [141]Country code top-level domain
   Status Active
   [142]Registry Island Networks
   Sponsor Island Networks
   Intended use Entities connected with [143]Jersey
   Actual use Popular in Jersey
   Registration restrictions None
   Structure Registrations are directly at second level or under second
   level generic categories
   Documents [144]T&C
   Dispute policies [145]DRS
   [146]DNSSEC Yes
   Registry website [147]Island Networks

   .je is the [148]country code top-level domain for [149]Jersey. The
   domain is administered by Island Networks, who also administer the
   [150].gg domain for neighbouring territory [151]Guernsey. In 2003, a
   [152]Google Search website was made available for Jersey, which uses
   the .je domain.^[153][1]^[154][2]^[155][3]

History

   [[156]edit]

   [157]Alderney hosts the [158]domain name registry for both
   [159]bailiwicks of the Channel Islands. The country-code top-level
   domains of .je and [160].gg first appeared on the Internet in August
   1996 after [161]Jon Postel agreed with [162]Nigel Roberts to add four
   codes (GG and JE, and IM and AC) to the [163]IANA list of TLDs. The
   codes for the Channel Islands and for the [164]Isle of Man were entered
   on to the official [165]United Nations [166]ISO-3166 list in 2006.

Second-level domains

   [[167]edit]

   Since 2000, names have been registered principally directly under the
   country code.

   However the following legacy sub-domain are still open for registration
     * .co.je: commercial/personal domains
     * .net.je: [168]Internet service providers and commercial
     * .org.je: organisations (free to local good causes)
     * .sch.je: schools, primary and secondary education
