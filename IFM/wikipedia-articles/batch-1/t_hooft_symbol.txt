   Mathematical symbol used in algebras

   The 't Hooft symbol is a collection of numbers which allows one to
   express the [56]generators of the [57]SU(2) Lie algebra in terms of the
   [58]generators of Lorentz algebra. The symbol is a blend between the
   [59]Kronecker delta and the [60]Levi-Civita symbol. It was introduced
   by [61]Gerard 't Hooft. It is used in the construction of the [62]BPST
   instanton.
