   100 Years Later is a 2016 British [64]documentary film directed,
   written, and produced by John Lubbock.^[65][1] The film follows the
   work of historian [66]Ara Sarafian, executive director of the
   [67]Gomidas Institute in [68]London, in his efforts to create dialogue
   in [69]Turkey among [70]Armenians, [71]Kurds, and [72]Turks on the
   occasion of the 100th anniversary of the [73]Armenian genocide on 24
   April 2015. The film includes appearances by [74]Selahattin Demirtaş
   and [75]İsmail Beşikçi.^[76][1]
