   [75]Infobox references

   Chemical compound

   1,2-Dioleoyl-sn-glycerophosphoethanolamine is a non-bilayer lipid of
   the [76]phosphatidylethanolamine class, it adopts non-lamellar reverse
   hexagonal structures.^[77][1] It forms part of [78]Lipofectamine, a
   common [79]transfection reagent.
