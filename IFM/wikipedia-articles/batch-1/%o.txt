   From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
   (Redirected from [127]%o)
   Unit for parts per thousand
   "%o", "‰", and "Promille" redirect here. For the Finnish video game,
   see [128]Promille (video game). For the format string specifiers, see
   [129]printf format string and [130]scanf format string. Not to be
   confused with [131]%, indicating [132]parts per hundred, or with
   [133]‱, indicating [134]parts per ten thousand. Not to be confused with
   [135]parts per million.

             ‰
         Per mille
   In [136]Unicode U+2030
   ‰ PER MILLE SIGN (&permil;)
   Related
   See also U+0025
   % [137]PERCENT SIGN
   U+2031 ‱ [138]PER TEN THOUSAND SIGN (Basis point)

   The phrase per mille (from [139]Latin [140]per [141]mīlle 'in each
   thousand')^[142][1] indicates [143]parts per thousand.^[144][2] The
   associated symbol is ‰, similar to a [145]per cent sign % but with an
   extra zero in the [146]divisor.

   Major dictionaries do not agree on the
   spelling,^[147][1]^[148][2]^[149][3] giving other options of per
   mil,^[150][2] per mill,^[151][1]^[152][3] permil,^[153][1]^[154][4]
   permill,^[155][1] permille.^[156][5]^[157][6]

   The word promille is the [158]cognate in Dutch, German, Finnish and
   Swedish, and is sometimes seen as a [159]loanword in English with the
   same meaning as per mille.^[160][7]^[161][4]

   The symbol is included in the [162]General Punctuation block of
   [163]Unicode at U+2030
   ‰ PER MILLE SIGN.^[164][5] There is also an Arabic-Indic per mille sign
   at U+0609 ؉ ARABIC-INDIC PER MILLE SIGN.

Examples

   [[165]edit]

Blood alcohol concentration

   [[166]edit]

   Main article: [167]Blood alcohol concentration

   Promille and permille are likely best known as shorthand terms for
   [168]Blood Alcohol Concentration, where 1 promille corresponds to 1
   gram of alcohol per liter of blood.^[169][8] Promille is used in the
   legal limits of [170]blood-alcohol content for driving a road vehicle
   in some countries: for example: 0.5‰ or 0.8‰.^[171][9]

Cost per mille

   [[172]edit]

Sales and marketing

   [[173]edit]

   Main article: [174]Cost per mille

   [175]Cost per mille (CPM), the price of 1000 units, may be used for
   views of banner and display advertising, and for emails delivered by
   [176]email service providers.

Insurance

   [[177]edit]

   In UK insurance usage, the cost per mille is the rate per £1000 of
   insured value.^[178][10] In India, the premium per mille is the rate
   expressed as thousandths of the sum assured.^[179][11]

Gradients

   [[180]edit]

   [181]A railroad distance and gradient sign in Gdańsk, Poland. The 50‰
   grade is equivalent to 5%. A tram and gradient sign in [182]Gdańsk,
   Poland. The 50‰ [183]grade is equivalent to 50 m/km or 5%.

   Main article: [184]Grade (slope)

   Gradients (in some countries in [185]Europe) may be expressed as mm/m
   or m/km, and written with the per mille symbol.^[186][12]^[187][13]

Isotope ratios

   [[188]edit]

   Per mille may be used to express [189]stable-isotope ratios—for
   example: "δ^13C was measured at −3.5‰"

Taxation

   [[190]edit]

Charity fraction

   [[191]edit]

   In Italy, it is possible to nominate a charity or charities to receive
   "five per mille" (5‰) of personal taxation payments.^[192][14]

Property tax

   [[193]edit]

   Main article: [194]Property tax

   [195]Property taxation rates may be expressed as the [196]millage rate
   ([197]U.S.) or mill rate ([198]Canada), from [199]mill (currency), one
   thousandth of a dollar.

Quantile

   [[200]edit]

   Main article: [201]Quantile

   Permille may also be used to express a [202]1000-quantile ("999th
   permille"),^[203][15]^[204][16] but this usage is rare and largely
   obsolete. Modern publications instead use fractional percentiles
   ("99.9th percentile").^[205][17]

Salinity

   [[206]edit]

   Main article: [207]Salinity

   Seawater [208]salinity is often expressed per mille. Average marine
   salinity is about 35 parts per thousand or 35‰ (3.5%).^[209][18]
