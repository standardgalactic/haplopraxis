   1,2-dihydroxynaphthalene dioxygenase ([80]EC [81]1.13.11.56, 1,2-DHN
   dioxygenase, DHNDO, 1,2-dihydroxynaphthalene oxygenase,
   1,2-dihydroxynaphthalene:oxygen oxidoreductase) is an [82]enzyme with
   [83]systematic name naphthalene-1,2-diol:oxygen
   oxidoreductase.^[84][1]^[85][2]^[86][3] This enzyme [87]catalyses the
   following [88]chemical reaction

          naphthalene-1,2-diol + O[2]
          [MATH: <semantics> <mrow class="MJX-TeXAtom-ORD"> <mstyle
          displaystyle="true" scriptlevel="0"> <mo class="MJX-variant"
          stretchy="false">⇌</mo> </mstyle> </mrow> <annotation
          encoding="application/x-tex">{\displaystyle \rightleftharpoons
          }</annotation> </semantics> :MATH]
          {\displaystyle \rightleftharpoons }
          2-hydroxy-2H-chromene-2-carboxylate

   This enzyme is involved in [89]naphthalene degradation.
