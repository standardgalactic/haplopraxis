   Internet country-code top level domain for the Northern Mariana Islands

   CAPTION: .mp

   [140][220px-DotMP_domain_logo.svg.png]
   Introduced October 22, 1996
   [141]TLD type [142]Country code top-level domain
   Status Active as of January 2009
   [143]Registry [144]Saipan DataCom, Inc.
   Sponsor [145]Saipan DataCom, Inc.
   Intended use Entities connected with  [146]Northern Mariana Islands
   Actual use Marketed as a domain for sites usable on mobile devices;
   sometimes used where MP stands for [147]Member of Parliament
   Registration restrictions No registration restrictions; purchased
   domains have no restrictions
   Structure Registrations are directly at second level; there are a few
   sites related to the Northern Mariana Islands in third level domains
   under such second level names as .gov.mp and .org.mp
   Documents [148]Registration and Service Agreement; [149]Cancellation
   and Transfer policy
   Dispute policies [150]UDRP Dispute Resolution Policy
   Registry website [151]get.mp

   .mp is the [152]Internet [153]country code [154]top-level domain
   ([155]ccTLD) for [156]Northern Mariana Islands. There are a few sites
   related to the Northern Mariana Islands in this domain (such as
   governmental sites under .gov.mp and a few sites in .org.mp and
   .co.mp). The get.mp site allows users to register and manage .mp
   domains. The .mp name comes from [157]ISO 3166^[158][1] which specifies
   MP as the two letter designation for the [159]Northern Mariana Islands.

Official registry site

   [[160]edit]

   Domain name registry services for .mp domains are operated by Saipan
   DataCom, a local ISP.^[161][2]^[162][3] Domains with four or more
   characters may be purchased for $20. Premium domains under four
   characters range in price, with two-character domains running $5,000
   for a three-year registration. There are some sites related to the
   Northern Mariana Islands under third-level domains .gov.mp and .co.mp.

Whois services

   [[163]edit]

   The MP Domain does not have public whois services available. Saipan
   DataCom will provide whois data in cases of potential trademark
   infringement.^[164][4] Although ICANN documents specify
   whois.nic.mp^[165][3] as of July 2018 this host does not resolve.

Chi.mp

   [[166]edit]

   [167]Saipan DataCom formerly operated chi.mp, a content hub and
   identity management platform providing .mp domains and websites. Old
   free .mp registrations provided earlier by chi.mp were terminated
   without a notice and the chi.mp web site has been defunct since at
   least May 2013^[[168]citation needed]. As of August 2021 get.mp is
   offering chi.mp for sale for $20,000.00.^[169][5] [170]Mailchimp uses
   .mp as a [171]domain hack (mailchi.mp) to redirect to its main website.
