   Internet country code top-level domain for Jordan

   CAPTION: .jo

   [146][220px-Arms_of_Jordan.svg.png]
   Introduced 23 November 1994 (in root zone)
   [147]TLD type [148]Country code top-level domain
   Status Active
   [149]Registry [150]Ministry of Digital Economy And Entrepreneurship
   Sponsor [151]NITC
   Intended use Entities connected with  [152]Jordan
   Actual use Used by Jordanian companies and organizations
   Registered domains 5,686 (2022-12-17)^[153][1]
   Registration restrictions Must submit official documents
   Structure Registrations are at second level or at third level beneath
   second level labels
   Documents [154]Registration policy
   Dispute policies Resolved by Jordanian Law
   Registry website [155]DNS

   .jo is the [156]country code top-level domain (ccTLD) for [157]Jordan.
   A local contact is required to register a domain name under .jo. It
   used to be administered by the NITC and is now administered by Jordan's
   [158]Ministry of Digital Economy and Entrepreneurship.^[159][2]

   [160]Jordan also has an [161]internationalized country code top-level
   domain, الاردن.^[162][3]

Structure

   [[163]edit]

   Registration directly at second level, under .jo is possible, but there
   is a number of second level domains used for registrations as
   well.^[164][3] Registration under the IDN الاردن. is open for all
   public and private organisations and companies.^[165][3]

   CAPTION: List of second level domains

   Second level domain                  Intended use
   .com.jo             Commercial organizations
   .edu.jo             Universities and educational institutions
   .sch.jo             Public and private schools
   .net.jo             Networks and Internet service providers
   .org.jo             Non-commercial or not-for-profit organisations
   .gov.jo             Governmental entities
   .mil.jo             Military entities
   .per.jo             Individuals and personal use
   .phd.jo             PhD holders
