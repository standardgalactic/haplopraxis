   Chemical compound

   11-Dehydrocorticosterone (11-DHC), also known as 11-oxocorticosterone
   or 17-deoxycortisone, as well as 21-hydroxypregn-4-ene-3,11,20-trione,
   is a [82]naturally occurring, [83]endogenous [84]corticosteroid related
   to [85]cortisone and [86]corticosterone.^[87][1] It is a potent
   [88]mineralocorticoid, with generally greater such activity than that
   of corticosterone.^[89][1]
