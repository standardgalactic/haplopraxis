   [66]Netherlands in the Eurovision Song Contest

                        [67]◄1964 • 1965 • [68]1966►

   The Netherlands was represented at the [69]Eurovision Song Contest 1965
   with the song "'t Is genoeg", composed by Johnny Holshuyzen, with
   lyrics by Karel Prior, and performed by [70]Conny Vandenbos. The Dutch
   participating Broadcaster, [71]Nederlandse Televisie Stichting (NTS),
   selected its entry through a national final.

   Five acts participated in the Dutch preselection, which consisted of
   five qualifying rounds, followed by the final on 13 February. All the
   shows were held at the Theater Concordia in [72]Bussum, hosted by the
   1959 Eurovision winner [73]Teddy Scholten. Vandenbos had previously
   taken part in the Dutch preselection of [74]1962. Future Dutch
   representative [75]Ronnie Tober ([76]1968) was one of the other
   participants.

Nationaal Songfestival 1965

   [[79]edit]

Heats

   [[80]edit]

   Five qualifying heats took place on consecutive evenings between 8 and
   12 February. Each involved one of the selected acts performing three
   songs, with the jury winner from each act going forward to the final.
   The 31-member jury contained a mixture of journalists, employees of
   organising NTS, and randomly chosen audience members.^[81][1] The same
   format was used again in the Dutch preselections of [82]1966 and
   [83]1996.

Final

   [[87]edit]

   The national final was held on 13 February. The winning song was again
   chosen by a mixed 31-member jury, each awarding 1 point to their
   favourite song. "'t Is genoeg" was the winner by a 5-point
   margin.^[88][2]

   CAPTION: Final - 13 February 1965

   Draw       Artist                    Song              Points Place
   1    Gert Timmerman      "Waar de wind de zomer vindt" 1      5
   2    [89]Conny Vandenbos "'t Is genoeg"                13     1
   3    Trea Dobbs          "Ploem ploem jenka"           6      3
   4    [90]Ronnie Tober    "Geweldig"                    8      2
   5    [91]Shirley         "Blijf bij mij"               3      4

At Eurovision

   [[92]edit]

   On the night of the final Vandenbos performed first in the running
   order, preceding the [93]United Kingdom. Voting was by each national
   jury awarding 5-3-1 to its top three songs, and at the close of the
   voting "'t Is genoeg" had received 5 points (all from [94]Norway),
   placing the Netherlands 11th of the 18 entries. The Dutch jury awarded
   its 5 points to contest winners [95]Luxembourg.^[96][3]

   The Dutch conductor at the contest was [97]Dolf van der Linden.

   The members of the Dutch jury were Annie Bosma-Banning, [98]Kitty
   Knappert [[99]nl], and [100]Frans Boelen [[101]nl].^[102][4]
