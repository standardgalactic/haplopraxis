   ...Continued is the second album released by [74]Tony Joe White. It was
   released on [75]Monument Records and contained the single
   "[76]Roosevelt and Ira Lee" It was recorded at Monument Studios,
   [77]Nashville and Lyn-Lou Studios, [78]Memphis in 1969. It was produced
   by [79]Billy Swan and engineered by Tommy Strong and Mort Thomasson.

   The album was re-released on by Movieplay/Intermusic from Portugal in
   1993 with a different cover and another title (Roosevelt And Ira Lee).
   In 1997 it was rereleased by [80]Warner Brothers containing two
   additional songs - "Watching The Trains Go By" (by Dewey Oldham and
   Wallace Pennington) and "Old Man Willis" (by Tony Joe White himself)
   was the second single. "Old Man Willis" was later re-recorded for the
   album.

   The album contained the track "[81]Rainy Night In Georgia" popularized
   by [82]R&B vocalist [83]Brook Benton in [84]1970. It reached #4 on the
   [85]Pop Singles and #2 on the [86]Adult Contemporary charts,
   respectively. The [87]RIAA certified the single gold for sales of one
   million copies. In 2004, it was ranked #498 on the [88]List of Rolling
   Stone's 500 Greatest Songs of All Time.

   The song has been [89]covered by a number of musicians, including
   [90]Ray Charles, [91]Otis Rush, [92]Randy Crawford, [93]Tennessee Ernie
   Ford, [94]Amos Garrett, [95]Hank Williams, Jr., [96]Shelby Lynne,
   [97]John Holt, [98]Nicky Thomas, by the duet of [99]Conway Twitty and
   [100]Sam Moore, [101]Aaron Neville, and reggae band [102]The
   Gladiators. Most recently [103]indie [104]folk-rock band [105]Hem
   released a cover on [106]No Word from Tom (2006). Australian band Ross
   Hanniford Trio recorded a cover of the song on their 1994 album.
   [107]David Ruffin recorded a cover version of the song the same year as
   Benton; however, [108]Motown for unknown reasons did not release the
   album. A dance version was recorded by [109]Boozoo Bajou in 2006.
   "Elements And Things" was covered by [110]Blues Pills on their second
   album [111]Lady In Gold.^[112][1]
