   Chemical compound

   1,1-Diethoxyethane ([93]acetaldehyde diethyl [94]acetal) is a major
   flavoring component of distilled beverages, especially [95]malt
   whisky^[96][3] and [97]sherry.^[98][4] Although it is just one of many
   compounds containing an acetal functional group, this specific chemical
   is sometimes called simply acetal.
