   Internet top-level domain

   CAPTION: .moe

  [66][220px-.moe_TLD_logo.png]
         Introduced         2013
        [67]Registry        [68]Interlink [[69]ja]
           Sponsor          None
        Intended use        The marketing of products or services deemed [70]moe
  Registration restrictions None
          Documents         [71]ICANN registry agreement
      Registry website      [72]nic.moe/en/

   The domain name .moe is a [73]top-level domain (TLD) in the [74]Domain
   Name System of the [75]Internet. Its name comes from the [76]Japanese
   [77]slang word [78]moe, indicating its intended purpose in the
   marketing of products or services deemed moe.^[79][1]

History

   [[80]edit]

   [81]Interlink [[82]ja] began developing the
   moe [83]top-level domain (TLD) in 2012. On November 13, 2013, [84]ICANN
   and Interlink entered into a [85]registry agreement under which
   Interlink operates the
   moe TLD.^[86][2] Interlink sponsored a contest held between April 11
   and May 6, 2014, to design the domain's logo.^[87][3]^[88][4] The
   general registration period began on July 22, 2014.^[89][1]
