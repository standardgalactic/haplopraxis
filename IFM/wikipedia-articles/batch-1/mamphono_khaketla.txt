   'Mamphono Khaketla

   'Mamphono Khaketla (born 5 March 1960) is a [66]Lesotho
   [67]mathematician and [68]senator who served as [69]Minister of Finance
   from March 2015 to June 2017.

Early life and education

   [[70]edit]

   Khaketla was born in [71]Maseru on 5 March 1960 to Bennett Makalo and
   [72]Caroline Ntseliseng ’Masechele Khaketla.^[73][1] Her father was a
   novelist, journalist, politician and former minister, as well as the
   major shareholder of Mohlabani Property Company, and left her a
   sizeable estate.^[74][2]^[75][3] Her mother was a teacher and author,
   one of the first women published in Lesotho.^[76][4]^[77][5]

   Khaketla did her primary and secondary schooling Maseru, before
   receiving a [78]Bachelor of Education from the [79]National University
   of Lesotho in 1980.^[80][4] She has a master's degree in education and
   a [81]PhD in [82]mathematics education from the [83]University of
   Wisconsin (1991).^[84][4] Her thesis was titled "An analysis of the
   Lesotho Junior Certificate Mathematics Examination and its impact on
   instructions".^[85][1]

Career

   [[86]edit]

   Khaketla was a lecturer in [87]mathematics at the National Teacher
   Training College from 1981 until 1995 and became assistant director of
   the college.^[88][4] She worked at the Institute of Development
   Management in Lesotho and [89]Botswana from 1996 until 2001 before
   becoming the director of the Centre for Accounting Studies.

   Khaketla was appointed as a senator by [90]Prime Minister [91]Pakalitha
   Mosisili in 2002 and served as Minister of Communications, Science and
   Technology from 2002 until 2004.^[92][4] At the [93]2007 election, she
   lost her seat but was elected to the [94]National Assembly as one of
   the [95]Lesotho Congress for Democracy members on a party list for
   [96]proportional representation submitted by the National Independence
   Party.^[97][4] She served as Minister of Education and Training from
   2007 until 2012. In 2011, Khaketla was one of seven women ministers in
   the Cabinet, alongside: [98]Mannete Ramali, [99]Maphoka Motoboli,
   [100]Mathabiso Lepono, [101]Mphu Keneileo Ramatlapeng, [102]Mpeo
   Mahase-Moiloa and [103]Pontso Suzan Matumelo Sekatle.^[104][6] On 30
   March 2015 she was appointed [105]Minister of Finance.

   In November 2015, she presided over the 102nd session of the
   [106]African, Caribbean and Pacific Group of States Council of
   Ministers.^[107][7]

   In July 2016, Khaketla was accused of soliciting a bribe for a major
   government contract in a case that was before the courts. She denied
   the allegation.^[108][8]^[109][9]
