   Swedish rapper (born 1997)

   1.Cuz
   [68]1.Cuz on mask, a photo taken in February 2020
   1.Cuz on mask, a photo taken in February 2020
   Background information
   Birth name Abas Abdikarim Bakar^[69][1]
   Born (1997-09-23) 23 September 1997 (age 27)
   [70]Jönköping, [71]Sweden
   Origin [72]Hässelby, [73]Stockholm, Sweden
   Genres [74]Hip hop
   Occupations Rapper
   Years active 2018–present
   Musical artist

   Abas Abdikarim Bakar, better known as 1.Cuz (born September 23, 1997),
   is a Swedish rapper of [75]Somali heritage from [76]Hässelby,
   [77]Stockholm.^[78][2] He is known for performing with a white
   [79]balaclava covering his head, and he has never revealed his real
   name to the public.^[80][3]

   1.Cuz spent two years in prison, an experience that he later said
   influenced his musical career.^[81][4] In November 2018, he released
   his debut single "Akta mannen", and in December 2019, his studio album
   1 År reached the top of the [82]Swedish Albums Chart.^[83][5] In 2022,
   1.Cuz was featured on a remix of [84]Ed Sheeran's song "[85]2step", and
   the two later performed together at the [86]Ullevi stadium.^[87][6]

Community engagement and business activities

   [[88]edit]

   Among other things, Bakar has collaborated with [89]Greta Thunberg, for
   example to call for vaccination against covid. Bakar has also become
   involved in the climate issue by performing at [90]Fridays for Future's
   events in 2020 and 2022.^[91][7]

   Bakar runs the company Pro Mr Investment AB for his activities as an
   artist.^[92][8]
