   From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

   For a list of paraphilias, see [62]List of paraphilias.
   Greek root used in English
   Look up [63]phil-, [64]-philia, [65]-phile, or [66]-philic in
   Wiktionary, the free dictionary.

   The Greek [67]root -phil- originates from the Greek word meaning
   "love". For example, [68]philosophy (along with the Greek root -soph-
   meaning "wisdom") is the study of human customs and the
   [69]significance of life. One of the most common uses of the root
   -phil- is with philias.

   A [70]philia is the [71]love or [72]obsession with a particular thing
   or subject. The suffix -philia is used to specify the love or obsession
   with something more specific. It is somewhat [73]antonymic to
   [74]-phobia. Philias can be biological (e.g. rhizophilia, preference
   for living on roots) or chemical (e.g. chromophilous, materials that
   stain easily), or can be a hobby/liking (e.g. icthyophilia, love for
   fish).

   Philia (φιλία) as a [75]Greek word for love refers to [76]brotherly
   love, including [77]friendship and [78]affection. This contrasts to the
   Greek terms [79]Eros, or [80]sexual/[81]romantic love, and [82]agape,
   or detached, spiritual love. However, English usage differs in some
   cases from the etymological use, and several of these words refer in
   English not to brotherly love but to sexual attraction.

   The suffix -phile (or, in a few cases, -philiac) applies to someone who
   has one of these philia. It is the antonym of -phobe.

   Phil- (philo-) may also be used as a prefix with a similar meaning.

Words that use -phil-

Biology

   [[84]edit]
     * [85]Acidophilia: preference of acidic conditions
     * [86]Alkaliphilia: preference of alkaline environments
     * [87]Anthophilia: attraction to flowers
     * [88]Anthropophile: attracted to humans (e.g., parasites)
     * [89]Anemophily: a form of pollination whereby pollen is distributed
       by wind
     * [90]Capnophile: microorganism that thrives in the presence of high
       concentrations of [91]carbon dioxide
     * [92]Cryophilia: preference for cold environments, climates, objects
       and low temperatures; e.g., [93]Protea cryophila (Snow Protea)
     * [94]Dacryphilia: attraction to tears
     * [95]Dendrophilia: preference of trees or other plants
     * [96]Entomophilous: adapted for pollination by insects
     * [97]Extremophilia: preference of living extremal conditions for
       some microorganisms
     * [98]Geophilia: preferring to organisms that prefer the soil
     * [99]Halophilia: attraction to salt or salt-water
     * [100]Heliophilia: attraction to sunlight
     * [101]Hydrophilia: attraction to water
     * [102]Hyperthermophilia: organisms that thrive in extremely hot
       environments
     * [103]Limnophilia: preference of ponds or marshes
     * [104]Lithophilia: preference or affinity to stones
     * [105]Mesophilia: preference of moderate temperatures in
       microorganisms
     * [106]Microaerophilia: organisms that can tolerate or require
       environments containing low levels of oxygen
     * [107]Myrmecophilia: love for the ants
     * Nemophilia: love of the woods and forests
     * [108]Necrophilia: an attraction to the dead
     * [109]Neutrophile: organism that thrives in a neutral pH environment
     * [110]Nyctophilia: an attraction to darkness or night; finding
       relaxation or comfort in the darkness. Preferring nighttime
       activities to daytime activities
     * Ombrophilia: affinity to large amounts of rainfall
     * [111]Pedophilia: Persistent sexual attraction to prepubescent
       children.
     * [112]Petrophilia: preference of living or spending time in rocky
       areas
     * [113]Photophilia: preference of living or spending time in lighted
       conditions
     * [114]Pluviophilia: affinity to rain
     * [115]Psammophile: a plant loving sandy areas
     * [116]Psychrophilia: preference of cold temperatures
     * [117]Rheophilia: preference of living in running water
     * [118]Sciaphilia: preference of living in shady or dark areas (as
       opposed to photophilia)
     * [119]Thermophilia: love of high temperatures; thriving in high
       temperatures (e.g. microbes)
     * Tropophilia: preference of seasonal extremes of climate
     * [120]Trichophilia: an attraction to hair or fur
     * [121]Vorarephilia: sexual attraction to being eaten by or eating,
       typically by living organisms.
     * [122]Xenophilia: an affection for unknown, foreign objects, or
       people
     * Xerophilia: love of living or spending time in very dry conditions
     * Xylophilia: love of wood
     * [123]Zoophily: a form of pollination whereby pollen is distributed
       by animals

Chemistry and physics

   [[124]edit]
     * [125]Electrophile: a chemical species that forms bonds with
       nucleophiles by accepting a pair of electrons
     * [126]Hydrophilic: (of a substance) having a tendency to interact
       with or be dissolved by water and other polar substances
     * [127]Lipophilic: solubility or solvability of a substance with
       lipids
     * [128]Lipophilic: (of a substance) in microfluidics, enriching on
       channel walls instead of in the middle of the channels (e.g. air
       bubbles)
     * [129]Nucleophile: a chemical species that donates an electron pair
       to form a chemical bond. Antonym: electrophile

Hobbies

   [[130]edit]
     * [131]Audiophilia: love of high-fidelity sound reproduction
     * [132]Arctophilia: love of [133]teddy bears; especially, an interest
       in collecting teddy bears
     * [134]Bibliophilia: love of books
     * [135]Cartophilia: love of [136]maps
     * [137]Cinephilia: love of cinema and film
     * [138]Philately: the study of stamps and postal history and other
       related items
     * [139]Glossophilia: love of languages (synonym for [140]philology)
     * [141]Logophilia: love of words — logophiles may be interested in
       word games, such as crosswords, or Scrabble, and in the extreme,
       derive enjoyment from reading things commonly given less notice,
       such as labels
     * [142]Metrophilia: love of the metro rail or subway systems
     * [143]Neophilia: love of the latest novelties and trends
     * [144]Oenophilia: love of wine
     * [145]Ornithophilia: love of birds
     * [146]Taphophilia: love of graves, cemeteries and funerals
     * [147]Technophilia: love of technology
     * [148]Videophile: person who is concerned with achieving
       high-quality results in the recording and playback of movies, TV
       programs, etc

National or ethnic

   [[149]edit]
     * [150]Anglophile: a non-English person who is extremely fond of all
       things [151]English. Antonym: [152]Anglophobe
     * [153]Australophile: a fan of Australian culture
     * [154]Austrophile: a fan of Austrian culture
     * [155]Europhile: a person who wants to increase cooperation between
       governments within the [156]European Union. Antonym:
       [157]Eurosceptic
     * [158]Fennophile: a fan of Finnish culture
     * [159]Francophile or Gallophile: a fan of French culture. Antonym:
       [160]Francophobe
     * [161]Germanophile or Teutophile: a fan of German culture. Antonyms:
       [162]Germanophobe and teutophobia
     * [163]Hellenophile: a fan of Greek culture (i.e. someone prone to
       [164]philhellenism)
     * [165]Hibernophile: a lover of [166]Ireland or [167]Irish culture
     * [168]Indophile: a fan of [169]India
     * [170]Italophile: a fan of [171]Italy. Antonym: [172]Italophobia
     * [173]Japanophile: a [174]non-Japanese person with a strong interest
       in [175]Japan or [176]Japanese culture. Antonym: [177]Japanophobe
     * [178]Judeophile: a lover of [179]Jews or [180]Jewish culture.
       Antonyms: Judeophobe and [181]antisemite
     * [182]Kartvelophile: an interest for Georgian culture
     * [183]Negrophilia: a term used in the 1920s and 30s for the interest
       in Europe for African and African-American culture
     * [184]Persophilia: a fan of Iranian culture
     * [185]Russophilia: love of Russia and Russians. Antonym:
       [186]Russophobe
          + [187]Galician Russophilia was a pro-Russian movement in
            [188]Galicia and Lodomeria in the late 19th Century
     * [189]Sinophile: a non-Chinese person with a strong interest in
       [190]China or [191]Chinese culture. Antonym: [192]Sinophobe
     * [193]Slavophile: a fan of Slavic culture
     * [194]Suecophile: someone with a great interest in the Swedish
       language and culture
     * [195]Turkophile: a fan of Turkey and Turkish culture

Sexual paraphilias

   [[196]edit]
     * [197]List of paraphilias
     * [198]Paraphilia (previously known as sexual perversion and sexual
       deviation) is the experience of intense sexual arousal to atypical
       objects, situations, fantasies, behaviors, or individuals. Such
       attraction may be labeled [199]sexual fetishism

Other

   [[200]edit]
     * [201]Biophilia hypothesis
     * [202]Erotophilia: a personality trait which assesses an
       individual's disposition to respond to sexual cues
     * [203]Haemophilia: a disease relating to blood clotting
     * [204]Homophile: a term used to refer to [205]gay people or those
       who supported [206]homosexuality, prior to [207]gay liberation and
       other [208]LGBT social movements. It was proposed as an alternative
       to homosexual, that would have a more positive connotation, before
       the word "gay" became dedicated for this purpose
     * [209]Pluviophile: love of the rain
     * [210]Retrophilia: love of things of the past
     * [211]Theophilia: love of a god
     * [212]Topophilia: a strong sense of place, which often becomes mixed
       with the sense of [213]cultural identity among certain people and a
       love of certain aspects of such a place
