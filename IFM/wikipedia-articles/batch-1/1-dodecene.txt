   Chemical compound

   1-Dodecene is an [112]alkene with the formula C[10]H[21]CH=CH[2],
   consisting of a chain of twelve carbon atoms ending with a [113]double
   bond. While there are many isomers of dodecene depending on which
   carbon the double bond is placed, this isomer is of greater commercial
   importance. It is classified as an [114]alpha-olefin. Alpha-olefins are
   distinguished by having a double bond at the primary or alpha (α)
   position. This location of a double bond enhances the reactivity of the
   compound and makes it useful for a number of applications, especially
   for the production of [115]detergents.^[116][4]

Production and reactions

   [[117]edit]

   1-Dodecene is commercially produced by [118]oligomerization of ethylene
   via a number of processes. In the [119]Shell Higher Olefin Process
   (SHOP), a nickel catalyst is employed. In processes developed by Gulf
   and by Ethyl Corporations, [120]triethylaluminium is the catalyst.
   Similar to the SHOP method, these processes rely on ethylene insertion
   into an [121]Al-alkyl bond competitively with [122]beta-hydride
   elimination to give the alpha-olefin, regenerating an aluminium
   hydride. Other processes have been developed.^[123][5]^[124][6]
