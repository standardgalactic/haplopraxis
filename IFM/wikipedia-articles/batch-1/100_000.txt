   100,000 (one hundred thousand) is the [131]natural number following
   [132]99,999 and preceding 100,001. In [133]scientific notation, it is
   written as 10^5.

Terms for 100,000

   In [134]Bangladesh, [135]India, [136]Pakistan and [137]South Asia, one
   hundred thousand is called a [138]lakh, and is written as 1,00,000. The
   [139]Thai, [140]Lao, [141]Khmer and [142]Vietnamese languages also have
   separate words for this number: แสน, ແສນ, សែន (all saen), and ức
   respectively. The [143]Malagasy word is hetsy.^[144][1]

   In [145]the Netherlands, a '[146]ton' is a colloquialism for a
   denomination of 100.000 monetary units. In the [147]guilders period a
   ton would denote 100.000 guilders. With the introduction of the euro, a
   ton would come to mean 100.000 euros. The usage is mostly limited to
   the financial sphere and the buying and selling of houses. It is not
   used in official settings because of the ambiguity with commonly used
   [148]metric tonne. While usage is common in the Netherlands, it sees
   almost no use in [149]Belgium.^[[150]citation needed]

   In [151]Cyrillic numerals, it is known as the legion (
   легион): [152][30px-Legion-1000000-Cyrillic.svg.png] or
   [153][25px-%D0%9D%D0%B5%D1%81%D0%B2%D0%B5%D0%B4%D1%8C.svg.png] .

Values of 100,000

   In [154]astronomy, 100,000 metres, 100 kilometres, or 100 km (62 miles)
   is the [155]altitude at which the [156]Fédération Aéronautique
   Internationale (FAI) defines [157]spaceflight to begin.

   In [158]paleoclimatology, the [159]100,000-year problem is a mismatch
   between the temperature record and the modeled [160]incoming solar
   radiation.

   In the [161]Irish language, [162]céad míle fáilte (
   pronounced [163][ˌceːd̪ˠ ˈmʲiːlʲə ˈfˠaːl̠ʲtʲə]) is a popular greeting
   meaning "a hundred thousand welcomes".

     * 923,521 = 31^4
     * 925,765 = [419]Markov number^[420][24]
     * 925,993 = Keith number^[421][15]
     * 950,976 = [422]harmonic divisor number^[423][8]
     * 956,619: 956619^2=915119911161, and only the digits 1, 5, 6 and 9
       are used in both this number and its [424]square.
     * 967,680 = [425]highly totient number^[426][5]
     * 970,299 = 99^3, the largest 6-digit cube
     * 998,001 = 999^2, the largest 6-digit square. The reciprocal of this
       number, in its expanded form, lists all three-digit numbers in
       order except 998.^[427][59]
     * 998,991 = largest [428]triangular number with 6 digits and the
       1413th triangular number
     * 999,983 = largest 6-digit prime number
     * 999,999 = repdigit. [429]Rational numbers with denominators 7 and
       13 have 6-digit [430]repetends when expressed in [431]decimal form,
       because 999999 is the smallest number one less than a power of 10
       that is divisible by 7 and by 13, and it is the largest number in
       English not containing the letter 'l' in its name.

Prime numbers

   There are 9,592 primes less than 10^5, where 99,991 is the largest
   prime number smaller than 100,000.

   Increments of 10^5 from 100,000 through a [432]one million have the
   following prime counts:
     * 8,392 primes between 100,000 and 200,000.^[433][b] This is a
       difference of [434]1,200 primes from the previous range.
          + 104,729 is the 10,000th prime, which is in this range.
          + 199,999 is prime.
     * 8,013 primes between 200,000 and 300,000.^[435][c] A difference of
       [436]379 primes from the previous range.
          + 224,737 is the 20,000th prime.
     * 7,863 primes between 300,000 and 400,000.^[437][d] A difference of
       [438]150 primes from the previous range.
          + 350,377 is the 30,000th prime.
     * 7,678 primes between 400,000 and 500,000.^[439][e] A difference of
       [440]185 primes from the previous range. Here, the difference
       increases by a count of [441]35.
          + 479,909 is the 40,000th prime.
     * 7,560 primes between 500,000 and 600,000.^[442][f] A difference of
       [443]118 primes from the previous range.
          + [444]7,560 is the twentieth highly composite number.^[445][11]
          + 599,999 is prime.
     * 7,445 primes between 600,000 and 700,000.^[446][g] A difference of
       [447]115 primes from the previous range.
          + 611,953 is the 50,000th prime.
     * 7,408 primes between 700,000 and 800,000.^[448][h] A difference of
       [449]37 primes from the previous range.
          + 700,001 and 799,999 are both prime.
          + 746,773 is the 60,000th prime.
     * 7,323 primes between 800,000 and 900,000.^[450][i] A difference of
       [451]85 primes from the previous range. Here, the difference
       increases by a count of [452]48.
          + 882,377 is the 70,000th prime.
     * 7,224 primes between 900,000 and [453]1,000,000.^[454][j] A
       difference of [455]99 primes from the previous range. The
       difference increases again, by a count of [456]14.
          + 900,001 is prime.

   In total, there are 68,906 prime numbers between 100,000 and
   1,000,000.^[457][60]
