   For the band from South Carolina, see [69]Hundredth (band).

   In [70]arithmetic, a hundredth is a single part of something that has
   been divided equally into a hundred parts.^[71][1] For example, a
   hundredth of 675 is 6.75. In this manner it is used with the prefix
   "[72]centi-" such as in [73]centimeter. A hundredth is also one
   [74]percent.

   A hundredth is the [75]reciprocal of 100.

   A hundredth is written as a [76]decimal fraction as 0.01, and as a
   [77]vulgar fraction as 1/100.^[78][2]

   “Hundredth” is also the [79]ordinal number that follows “ninety-ninth”
   and precedes “hundred and first.” It is written as 100th.
